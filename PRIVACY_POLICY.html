Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 23, 2026

Overview

CoWriterAI is a privacy-first AI writing assistant. We believe your writing is private and treat it that way.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your text content — Text is only sent to the AI when you explicitly press the hotkey or click an action button. It is processed transiently and never stored on our servers.
  • Browsing history — We do not track or record which websites you visit.
  • Personal information — We do not collect names, emails, or any identifying information.
  • Passwords or payment info — The extension automatically skips password and payment fields.

What We Do Collect

  • Style preferences (local only) — When you accept an AI suggestion, a brief record is stored in chrome.storage.local on your device. This data never leaves your browser.
  • Anonymous client ID — A randomly generated UUID used solely for JWT authentication with the gateway. Not linked to any personal information.
  • Usage counters — The gateway tracks aggregate request counts per client ID for rate limiting. No text content is logged.

How AI Processing Works

When you trigger an AI action:

  • Your selected text is sent over HTTPS to the CoWriterAI gateway.
  • The gateway forwards it to the configured LLM provider (Groq).
  • The AI suggestion is returned to your browser.
  • No text is logged, stored, or retained at any step.

Third-Party Services

Groq (LLM Provider) — CoWriterAI uses the Groq API for fast AI inference. Groq's API terms state:

  • Groq does not use your prompts to train AI models.
  • Text is processed ephemerally — not stored in a database.
  • API usage provides enterprise-grade privacy vs. consumer web interfaces.

Stripe (Payments) — Pro subscription billing is handled by Stripe. Stripe's privacy policy governs payment data. We never see or store your card details.

Data Storage

All user preferences are stored locally in chrome.storage.local:

  • Gateway URL and hotkey configuration
  • Style profile (last 100 accepted suggestions)
  • Authentication token

Your Rights

  • Delete your data: Click "Clear Style Profile" in the extension popup, or uninstall the extension.
  • Self-host: Run the gateway on your own server for complete data control.
  • Inspect: All code is open source and auditable on GitHub.

Contact

For privacy questions, email jpgalik@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub.
