Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

CoWriterAI is a privacy-first AI writing assistant. We believe your writing is private and treat it that way.

What We Collect

  • Writing text when you request a feature — Selected text is sent to our gateway when you run an AI action. Nearby document context is sent only if you enable that setting. Text in supported fields is sent for live grammar checks only if you turn that feature on. The extension attempts to skip password, payment, and sensitive-looking fields.
  • Local preferences and style signals — When you accept a suggestion, the extension stores the action, time, and source/accepted text snippets (up to 160 characters each, with up to 24 saved entries) in chrome.storage.local. Lightweight style/tone signals learned from live grammar are also stored locally. Live grammar and nearby-document context are off by default.
  • Sign-in information — Guest mode uses a random client ID. If you sign in with email or Google, we store your email and an account identifier derived from it. Google may also provide your name and profile image. Google data is used to authenticate you and provide account features.
  • Cloud style sync — If you sign in and use this feature, accepted-edit preference snippets are sent to and stored by our gateway for access across devices. Live-grammar style/tone signals remain local.
  • Billing information — Stripe processes payments. We store subscription status and Stripe customer/subscription identifiers needed to provide and manage Pro access; we do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Usage events — We record sign-in, checkout-start, Pro activation, and billing-portal events against a pseudonymous account identifier for unique counts and conversion measurement. These analytics records expire after up to 400 days. Request counts are also used for rate limits.
  • Operational logs — Our gateway logs request method, API route, response status, duration, and IP address for operation and security. It does not log request bodies or writing text.

How AI Processing Works

When you request an AI action or enable live grammar, relevant text is sent over HTTPS to the CoWriterAI gateway and forwarded to our configured AI provider (Groq) to return a result. The gateway is designed not to log request bodies or writing text. The AI provider processes the text under its own terms and privacy practices. Do not use the live grammar feature with confidential writing unless you are comfortable sending that text for processing.

Third-Party Services

  • Groq processes text to provide AI suggestions.
  • Google Sign-In authenticates users who choose it and supplies basic profile information.
  • Resend delivers email sign-in codes.
  • Stripe processes subscription payments and billing management.
  • Render and Upstash host the gateway and store account, style-sync, rate-limit, and subscription data.

Data Storage and Deletion

Extension settings, authentication tokens, and local style preferences are stored in chrome.storage.local. Account records and cloud-synced style profiles are stored on the gateway infrastructure; one-time email codes expire after 15 minutes. Use "Clear Style Profile" in the popup to remove saved local style preferences and samples. Uninstalling the extension removes its local data from that browser. To request deletion of server-side account data, email moeohana808@gmail.com from your account address. We will verify the request and delete the associated account record and synced style profile. Payment records may need to be retained by Stripe or for legal and financial obligations.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

CoWriterAI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google account data is used only to authenticate you and provide requested account features. It is not sold or used for advertising, and is shared only as needed to provide those features, for security, or as required by law.

Contact

For privacy questions, email moeohana808@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub.
