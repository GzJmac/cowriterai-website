FAQ

Common questions

Does CoWriterAI see everything I type?

No. Your text is only sent to our servers when you explicitly trigger an action (by pressing Alt+Shift+A and choosing a transformation). We never monitor, log, or auto-scan what you type.

What AI model powers it?

CoWriterAI uses Llama 3.1 via the Groq API — a fast, capable open-weight model. Your text is sent to Groq's servers only at the moment you request a transformation, and is not used to train any models.

What's included in the Free tier?

20 AI actions per day — all 9 writing transformations, live grammar check, and style learning. The limit resets every day at midnight UTC. No credit card required.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Install the extension, sign in with Google or email in the Account tab, then click Upgrade to Pro in the Billing tab. You'll be taken to a secure Stripe checkout. Pro activates instantly.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Yes. Open the extension → Billing → Manage Subscription to access the Stripe customer portal and cancel with one click. No questions asked, no fees.

Does it work on Google Docs / Gmail / LinkedIn?

Yes — CoWriterAI works on virtually every website with a text field. This includes Gmail, Google Docs, Notion, Slack, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Reddit, HubSpot, and more.
